## 11. AI Crew

- This is an experimental feature; it is recommended to use only friendly, hostile, and faction gunners.
- Supports seat selection and configuration-mode switching.
- AI crew can drive vehicles. Friendly gunners attack monsters and vehicles driven by hostile gunners; hostile gunners attack players, neutral creatures, and vehicles driven by friendly gunners; faction gunners attack players and gunners not in the same faction.
